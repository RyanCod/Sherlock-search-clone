FAQ

Common questions.

How does Sleuth find someone from a photo?

Sleuth sends your photo to our search provider, which finds publicly available images similar to it — across social platforms and public records — then ranks them by similarity. A real search runs every time, and nothing is faked.

What happens to the photo I upload?

Your photo goes straight to our search provider to run that one search — Sleuth doesn't store it. We never add it to a public index, attach it to anyone else's profile, or sell it.

Are my search results public?

No. Your results are private to your account and only you can see them. We don't publish pages about the people you look up.

Is Sleuth legal to use?

Sleuth searches publicly available information for personal safety and verification. You're responsible for using the results lawfully. It isn't a consumer reporting tool, so it can't be used for employment, housing, or credit decisions.

Which platforms does Sleuth search?

We cover the major social networks, including Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, X, LinkedIn, YouTube, Pinterest, and OnlyFans, plus public records. We add more sources over time.
